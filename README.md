"Steps on How to use the Web"

1. Signup the email for Admin.
2. Signup the email for User.
3. Log in the email for User.
4. Click the profile on the navigational bar to see drop down and click profile setting.
5. Click the update profile and fill up the form.
6. Go Back to home page and click Book Now.
7. Fill up the form for appointment scheduled.
8. Go to appointments to see all the pending and approved appointment.
9. To approved the appointment, Log in the email for admin.
10. Go to appointment list, click the approved and fill up the assigned doctor / room..
11. To add doctor, go to doctors and hit add doctors.
